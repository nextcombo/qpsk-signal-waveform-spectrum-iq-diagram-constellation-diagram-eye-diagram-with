# QPSK signal waveform, spectrum, IQ diagram, constellation diagram, eye diagram [with MATLAB code]

1. QPSK Signal Principle
In the modulation methods of digital signals, Quadrature Phase Shift Keying (QPSK) is currently the most commonly used satellite digital signal modulation method. It has a high spectral utilization rate, strong anti-interference capability, and is also relatively simple to implement in circuits.

QPSK Signal Generation Process: The baseband coded waveform is mapped through QPSK (serial-to-parallel conversion divided into I and Q channels, then followed by level conversion, where 0 is converted to 1 and 1 is converted to -1), resulting in the QPSK modulated signal. This signal is then multiplied with the corresponding carrier, and finally added to complete the QPSK modulation.

The parameters include the carrier frequency, IQ representation of the QPSK modulation code, the bit width, and the waveform determined by the shaping filter.

Matched filtering is applied to the downconverted baseband signal, followed by sampling and decision-making to recover the original coded sequence.
